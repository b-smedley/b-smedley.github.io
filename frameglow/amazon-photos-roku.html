How to show Amazon Photos on Roku

There's no Amazon Photos channel for Roku, and Roku's own photo screensaver doesn't connect to it. Here are the ways to get your photos on the TV anyway.

The short answer

Amazon makes Amazon Photos for its own Fire TV and Echo Show devices, not for Roku. Roku's Backdrops feature (which replaced Photo Streams) can show your photos as the screensaver, but it connects to Google Photos, not Amazon Photos. To see Amazon Photos on a Roku you can:

  • Mirror your phone to show a few photos right now.
  • Upload copies to Roku Backdrops to use them as the screensaver.
  • Use Frameglow for Roku, which plays Amazon Photos shared albums and can be your Roku screensaver.

1. Mirror your phone

Open Amazon Photos on your phone and send the screen to the TV. From an iPhone or iPad, use Screen Mirroring in Control Center; many recent Roku TVs and players support AirPlay. From an Android phone, use its Cast or Screen mirroring option.

Good for: showing a few photos to people in the room. Not so good for: leaving it running. Your phone has to stay on and unlocked, photos are sized for a phone screen, and notifications show up on the TV.

2. Upload copies to Roku Backdrops

Download the photos you want from Amazon Photos, then upload them to Roku's Backdrops feature and choose Backdrops as your screensaver.

Good for: a small, fixed set of photos. Not so good for: albums that keep growing. Nothing stays in sync with Amazon, so new photos have to be downloaded and uploaded again by hand, and Backdrops shows one album at a time.

3. Use Frameglow for Roku

Frameglow plays Amazon Photos shared albums on your TV, full screen with slow pan and zoom, plus the time and the date each photo was taken. On Roku it also works as a screensaver: pick Frameglow in Roku's screensaver settings and your photos come on by themselves whenever the TV is idle. Photos added to the album later show up on their own, and you never type an Amazon password on the TV.

Coming soon to Roku

Frameglow is also made for Apple TV. The Roku version is in testing.

How setup will work

All you need is an Amazon Photos album and your phone.

  1. Share an album in Amazon PhotosIn the Amazon Photos app or website, open an album, choose Share, then Copy link.
  2. Open Frameglow and choose Add AlbumThe TV shows a QR code and a short code.
  3. Scan the code with your phoneA page opens where you paste the link. Your phone can be on any network, even mobile data.
  4. Tap Send to TVThe album appears a moment later. Add as many as you like.
  5. Make it your screensaverOn the Roku, go to Settings → Theme → Screensavers and choose Frameglow.

Questions

Can Amazon Photos be my Roku screensaver?

Not directly, since Backdrops doesn't connect to Amazon Photos. Frameglow for Roku adds a screensaver that plays your Amazon Photos shared albums whenever the Roku is idle.

Do I have to sign in to Amazon on the Roku?

No. Frameglow uses the album's share link, so there's no Amazon password to type and nothing is linked to your Amazon account.

Can I show my whole library?

Frameglow plays albums shared by link. Put the photos you want in an album, share it, and add that link. Add as many albums as you like and choose which ones play.

Can I show photos someone else shared with me?

Yes. If a friend or relative sends you an Amazon Photos share link, add it the same way. It's a good way to put a family album on a grandparent's TV.

Is this an official Amazon or Roku app?

No. Frameglow is made by an independent developer and isn't affiliated with Amazon or Roku. It works with Amazon Photos albums from amazon.com; albums from other Amazon countries haven't been tested yet.
