Sending to the TV showing

Where do I find the link?

In the Amazon Photos app or website, open an album, tap Share, then Copy link. It looks like https://www.amazon.com/photos/shared/…

Copying the address from your browser's address bar won't work: that page needs your Amazon sign-in. Use the share link.

Pasting the whole message the app gives you works too. Frameglow picks out the links.

Does it work with Google Photos, iCloud, or other services?

Not yet. Frameglow only works with Amazon Photos shared albums. Links from Google Photos, iCloud, Dropbox, Facebook, or anywhere else won't work.

The album must be shared with a link. Private albums and links that need your Amazon sign-in can't be shown on the TV.

Who can see my photos?

Anyone who has a share link can open it, without signing in, and download the full-size originals. Originals can include where each photo was taken.

Make an album just for your TV with the photos you want on it, and share that, rather than sharing your whole library. Only send the link to people you trust.

To stop a link working, turn off sharing for it in Amazon Photos.

The link you send passes through Frameglow's server only until your TV picks it up, and is deleted then. See the privacy policy.

Adding several albums at once

Paste as many links as you like, one per line, and send them together. Notes is a handy place to collect links before sending them.

You don't have to make an album first: select photos in Amazon Photos, tap Share, then Copy link, and paste that here.
