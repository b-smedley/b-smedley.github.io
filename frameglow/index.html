Your TV, as a photo frame

Frameglow plays your Amazon Photos shared albums on Apple TV as a slow, full-screen slideshow, with the time, the date each photo was taken, and the weather.

Coming soon to the App Store for Apple TV

Works only with albums shared by link from Amazon Photos.

Add an album from your phone

No account to sign in to on the TV, and nothing to type with the remote.

  1. Copy a share linkIn Amazon Photos, open an album, choose Share, then Copy link.
  2. Scan the code on your TVYour phone opens a page served by your Apple TV, on your own Wi-Fi.
  3. Paste and sendThe album appears on the TV a moment later. Add as many as you like.

Made to sit in the background

Quiet enough to leave on all evening, with just enough on screen to be useful.

Slow pan and zoom

Each photo drifts gently and crossfades into the next. Portrait photos sit over a soft blurred backdrop, not black bars.

Clock and weather

The time and current conditions for where you are, tucked in the corner.

When it was taken

The date, the album, and details like "3 years ago today".

Stays up to date

Photos added to a shared album show up on their own. If the internet drops, it keeps playing the photos it already has.

Your pace

Shuffle or play in date order, show each photo for 5 seconds to 5 minutes, and stop automatically after a few hours.

Browse too

Look through any album as a grid, view photos full screen, play videos, and see your photos in the Top Shelf.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your photos go from Amazon to your Apple TV and nowhere else. Location is used only for the weather, and it's optional. Read the privacy policy.
