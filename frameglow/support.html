Help with Frameglow, the photo frame app for Apple TV

Frameglow plays a slow, full-screen slideshow of your photos from Amazon Photos share links, with a clock, the date each photo was taken, and the local weather.

Getting started

  1. In the Amazon Photos app or website, open an album, choose Share, then Copy link. The link looks like https://www.amazon.com/photos/shared/…
  2. Open Frameglow on your Apple TV and scan the QR code with your phone. Your phone must be on the same Wi-Fi network as the Apple TV.
  3. Paste the link and tap Send to TV.

You can also type the link on the Apple TV itself. To see the app before adding anything, choose Watch a Sample Slideshow.

Common questions

The QR code page won't open on my phone

Check that the phone is on the same Wi-Fi network as the Apple TV and not on mobile data or a guest network. If tvOS asked whether Frameglow may find devices on your local network and you declined, allow it in Apple TV Settings → General → Privacy & Security → Local Network.

Frameglow says my link isn't right

It needs a share link to an album, not the address of your own Amazon Photos library. Create one with Share → Copy link. Anyone with the link can view the album, so share it only with people you trust.

New photos aren't showing up

Albums refresh every few hours and when you open the app. To refresh right away, go to Settings → Refresh all albums.

The weather isn't showing

Weather needs Location Services. On the Apple TV, go to Settings → General → Privacy & Security → Location Services, turn it on, and allow Frameglow.

How do the free trial and unlock work?

Browsing and adding albums is always free. Ambient mode and slideshows are free for 7 days, then need a one-time purchase. There is no subscription and nothing is charged automatically. If you reinstall or get a new Apple TV, choose Restore Purchases in Settings.

Remote controls

During a slideshow: swipe left or right to change photos, click or press Play/Pause to pause, swipe down for photo details, and press Back to exit.
