Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Frameglow is a photo frame app for Apple TV. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer receives no information about you or your photos. The only server the developer runs passes album links from your phone to your TV and deletes them straight away (see below).

What stays on your Apple TV

  • Album links and settings. The share links you add and your slideshow settings are saved on the Apple TV. Removing an album or deleting the app removes them.
  • Photo cache. Photos are downloaded to a temporary cache on the Apple TV so they load quickly and keep playing if the connection drops. You can clear it in Settings, and tvOS may clear it on its own.
  • Top Shelf. A short list of photos from your albums is shared with tvOS on the device so it can show them on the Home Screen.

What leaves your Apple TV

  • Amazon Photos. Frameglow loads your photos directly from Amazon using the share link you provide. It does not sign in to your Amazon account. Amazon receives the same information any web browser opening that link would send, such as your IP address. Amazon's handling of it is covered by Amazon's privacy notice.
  • Weather (optional). If you turn on the weather overlay and allow location access, Frameglow sends your Apple TV's approximate location to Apple's weather service to get current conditions. The location is kept on the device for a few hours to avoid asking again, and is used for nothing else. You can turn Weather off in Settings or withdraw location access in Apple TV Settings at any time.
  • Purchases. The free trial and the one-time unlock are handled by the App Store. The developer never sees your payment details. Apple provides the developer with aggregate sales reports only.

Adding an album from your phone

The Add Album screen shows a QR code and a short code. Scanning it opens a page on this website where you paste a share link. The link is sent to Frameglow's relay server (hosted on Cloudflare), held there only until your Apple TV collects it, usually within a few seconds, and then deleted. The result your phone shows (such as the album name) is deleted within 10 minutes. Codes expire after 10 minutes without use. The relay keeps no logs of links, and nothing it handles is linked to you, your device or your Apple ID. Cloudflare processes connections to the relay, including IP addresses, under Cloudflare's own privacy policy.

Children

Frameglow does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
