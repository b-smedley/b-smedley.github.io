How to show Amazon Photos on Apple TV

There's no Amazon Photos app for Apple TV. Here are three ways to get your photos on the TV anyway, from a quick mirror to a slideshow that runs all evening.

The short answer

Amazon makes Amazon Photos for its own Fire TV and Echo Show devices, not for Apple TV. The Photos app built into Apple TV shows only iCloud photos. To see Amazon Photos on an Apple TV you can:

  • Mirror your iPhone to show a few photos right now.
  • Copy the photos into iCloud so Apple's own Photos app can show them.
  • Use Frameglow, an Apple TV app that plays Amazon Photos shared albums as a full-screen slideshow.

1. Mirror your iPhone

Open Amazon Photos on your iPhone or iPad, open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring, and choose your Apple TV. Whatever is on your phone's screen appears on the TV.

Good for: showing a few photos to people in the room. Not so good for: leaving it running. Your phone has to stay on and unlocked, photos are sized for a phone screen, and notifications show up on the TV.

2. Copy the photos into iCloud

Download the photos you want from Amazon Photos, save them to the Photos app on your iPhone or Mac, and put them in an iCloud Shared Album. Apple TV's Photos app can then show them, and you can pick that album as the Apple TV screensaver.

Good for: a small, fixed set of photos. Not so good for: albums that keep growing. Nothing stays in sync, so new photos have to be copied over by hand, and they take up iCloud storage as well as Amazon storage.

3. Use Frameglow

Frameglow is an Apple TV app that plays Amazon Photos shared albums directly. Photos show full screen with slow pan and zoom, plus the time, the date each photo was taken and the weather. Photos added to the album later show up on their own, and you never type an Amazon password on the TV.

Set it up in about a minute

All you need is an Amazon Photos album and your phone.

  1. Share an album in Amazon PhotosIn the Amazon Photos app or website, open an album, choose Share, then Copy link.
  2. Open Frameglow and choose Add AlbumThe TV shows a QR code and a short code.
  3. Scan the code with your phoneA page opens where you paste the link. Your phone can be on any network, even mobile data.
  4. Tap Send to TVThe album appears a moment later. Choose it, or start ambient mode to play every album in the rotation.

Questions

Do I have to sign in to Amazon on the Apple TV?

No. Frameglow uses the album's share link, so there's no Amazon password to type and nothing is linked to your Amazon account.

Can I show my whole library?

Frameglow plays albums shared by link. Put the photos you want in an album, share it, and add that link. Add as many albums as you like and choose which ones play.

Can I show photos someone else shared with me?

Yes. If a friend or relative sends you an Amazon Photos share link, add it the same way. It's a good way to put a family album on a grandparent's TV.

Does it play videos?

Videos play when you browse an album and open them. The slideshow shows photos only.

What does it cost?

Adding and browsing albums is always free. Slideshows are free for 7 days, then a one-time purchase keeps them for good. No subscription.

Is this an official Amazon app?

No. Frameglow is made by an independent developer and isn't affiliated with Amazon. It works with Amazon Photos albums from amazon.com; albums from other Amazon countries haven't been tested yet.
